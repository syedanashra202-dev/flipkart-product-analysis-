# Flipkart Product Listings Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 8,000 Flipkart-style product listings using MySQL. The goal is to explore category-level pricing, discounts, product ratings, review counts, seller performance, and stock availability.

## Tools Used
- MySQL
- SQL
- Excel (dashboard)

## Dataset
The dataset contains 8,000 product listings and 11 columns, including:
- Product ID and product name
- Category and brand
- MRP and selling price
- Discount percentage
- Rating and review count
- Seller and stock availability

## Key Business Questions
1. Which categories have the most product listings?
2. Which categories have the highest average selling prices?
3. How do discounts vary across categories?
4. Which brands have high average ratings?
5. What proportion of listings are marked in stock?
6. How do sellers compare on stock availability?
7. Which products combine high listed discounts with strong ratings?

## Key Findings
- Mobiles has the highest number of listings, with 1,471 products.
- Mobiles has the highest average selling price, at ₹17,851.05.
- Beauty has the highest calculated average discount, at 22.95%.
- A total of 7,290 listings are marked in stock, representing 91.13%.
- Smart Deals has the highest seller in-stock percentage, at 91.87%.
- The high-discount query identifies listings with discounts of at least 40% and ratings of at least 4.0.
